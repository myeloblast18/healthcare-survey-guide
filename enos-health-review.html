Enos Health FAQ

Does Enos Health pay if you screen out?

Enos says it sends $5 when you are ineligible after five qualifying questions. Check the specific invitation; this does not cover every early exit.

How does Enos Health pay?

Its current site lists PayPal, Venmo, and paper check.

Is Enos Health legitimate?

Enos operates a healthcare survey website that publishes payment information and professional registration requirements. Its public signup form requests professional identification, such as an NPI or GMC ID. Those disclosures help you evaluate the service; they do not guarantee a specific income or study invitation.

When does Enos pay for a screen-out?

The published condition is specific: if you are ineligible after answering five qualifying questions, Enos says it sends $5. An invitation closing, an incomplete response or a different early exit should not automatically be treated as a payable screen-out. Check the invitation’s terms.
