ZoomRx FAQ

How does ZoomRx pay?

ZoomRx says it pays instantly via PayPal after completed surveys.

Is ZoomRx only for physicians?

ZoomRx markets its panel to healthcare professionals; exact eligibility depends on current registration and study criteria.

Does ZoomRx pay for screen-outs?

Published third-party panel guides now describe compensation for screening attempts. The general ZoomRx panel page linked below emphasizes instant PayPal payment but does not specify a universal screen-out amount or rule. Check the current invitation and account terms instead of assuming every attempt is paid or unpaid.

Why consider the mobile experience?

ZoomRx’s panel page describes recording voice responses and resuming a survey after an interruption. These features may help clinicians who complete research during short breaks. They do not establish a higher hourly return: include unsuccessful screening time in your own calculation.
