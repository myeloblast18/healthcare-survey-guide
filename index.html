What are paid medical surveys?

Healthcare market-research firms recruit qualified clinicians and other HCPs to answer questions about treatments, devices, clinical workflows, health technology, pharmaceutical products and other healthcare topics. Invitations and honoraria vary by specialty, geography, experience and the needs of each study.
