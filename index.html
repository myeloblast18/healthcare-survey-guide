What are paid healthcare surveys?

Healthcare market-research studies collect professional opinions about treatments, products, clinical workflows, medical devices, health technology and other healthcare topics. Depending on the study, researchers may recruit physicians, nurses, advanced practice providers, pharmacists, dentists, administrators, allied health professionals, patients or caregivers.

Participation is not the same as providing patient care. Reputable panels may verify professional credentials and typically match invitations to a participant's role, specialty and experience. Never disclose identifiable patient information in a market-research response.
