What counts as a paid healthcare survey?

Healthcare market research recruits qualified participants to answer questions about treatments, medical devices, clinical workflows, health technology, pharmaceutical products, professional education, and other healthcare topics. Studies can include online surveys, interviews, advisory discussions, polls, and longitudinal communities.

A legitimate panel may verify professional credentials and screen participants for individual studies. Eligibility for one survey does not imply eligibility for every survey, and honoraria vary substantially by role, specialty, geography, length, and recruiting difficulty.
