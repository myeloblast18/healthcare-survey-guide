A quick way to decide where to register

  1. Confirm eligibility: Check whether your profession, geography, career stage, and specialty are supported.
  2. Check screen-out treatment: The biggest advertised honorarium is not necessarily the best effective hourly rate.
  3. Review payout terms: Look at minimum redemption amounts, method, timing, and any conditions.
  4. Use the referral or promotional link before registering: Offers may require a first-time account, professional verification, or a qualifying action.
  5. Track actual earnings: After a few weeks, compare compensated minutes against all time spent on invitations and screeners.

Frequently asked questions

How much can a doctor earn from medical surveys?

There is no dependable universal amount. Earnings depend on specialty, study demand, screening success, honorarium, and time spent. Compare your actual return using the earnings calculator rather than relying on headline rates.

Are paid physician surveys legitimate?

Many established market-research firms recruit qualified clinicians. Verify professional requirements, payment terms, privacy policies, and the company's official registration domain before providing credentials.

Do doctors get paid when they screen out?

Policies vary considerably. Some panels publish limited screen-out compensation while others primarily pay for completed eligible studies. Review each invitation's terms.

Can I sign up for more than one panel?

Generally, yes, subject to each provider's account and eligibility rules. Never create duplicate accounts with the same provider.
