Which paid medical survey sites compensate screen-outs?

Screening friction is one of the biggest complaints HCPs have about survey panels. Here is the clearest policy information for the panels in this guide.

PanelPublished screen-out policyPractical implication
Enos Health$5 publicly stated after certain qualifying screen-outs; contributor reports $5–10 per screened-out survey.Join Enos
M3 Global ResearchM3 says it does not normally compensate screen-outs; unusually long screeners may be compensated when the invitation says so.Read M3 review
ZoomRxNo general public screen-out payment guarantee identified on the current panel page.Read ZoomRx review
SermoNo universal screen-out payment promise stated on the main earning page; incentives vary by opportunity.Read Sermo review

How to waste less time

  • Keep your professional profile accurate and current.
  • Respond early when a study is a good fit.
  • Prioritize invitations that disclose length and honorarium up front.
  • Track your own qualification rate by company.
  • Never misrepresent credentials or patient experience to qualify.
