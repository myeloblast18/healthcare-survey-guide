M3 Global Research FAQ

Does M3 Global Research pay for screen-outs?

M3 says it does not normally offer compensation for respondents who screen out, though some unusually long screeners may be compensated if stated in the invitation.

How quickly does M3 pay?

M3 says compensation is typically credited to its Digital Wallet within two business days after study completion, though some studies can take longer.

Is M3 Global Research a legitimate paid survey company?

M3 Global Research runs a healthcare market-research panel and publishes information on study payments and professional verification. Completing a registration does not guarantee invitations or earnings.

Does M3 pay everyone who starts a survey?

No. M3 says that it does not normally compensate screen-outs; some unusually long screeners may be exceptions if the invitation says so.
