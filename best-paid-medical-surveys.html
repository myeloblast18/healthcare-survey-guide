Enos Health

Officially verified: instant paymentsOfficial: at least $5 after qualifying questionsContributor: $5–10 screen-out reimbursement per surveyContributor: frequent surveys / strong honoraria

Enos Health is a U.S. healthcare-professional survey panel. Its official site states that invitations show the topic, time, and payment amount; completed studies are paid instantly via PayPal, Venmo, or paper check. Enos publicly states a $5 payment after certain qualifying screen-outs; the contributor reports receiving $5–10 in screen-out reimbursement per survey.

Eligibility note: Enos states that membership is currently open to U.S. health professionals. The contributor reports that the panel can be friendly to trainees, board-eligible physicians, and/or non-physician healthcare providers; verify your own eligibility during registration.

How this ranking should be used

There is no universal “highest-paying” healthcare survey company. Invitations are matched to research needs, so an oncologist, pharmacist, nurse practitioner, psychiatrist, surgeon, resident, or other HCP may have a completely different experience. The most sensible strategy is usually to join several legitimate panels and compare your own invitation rate, time-to-complete, screen-out experience, and effective hourly compensation.
