Which should you try first?

Choose ZoomRx first if mobile convenience is your priority. Its public page specifically describes resuming interrupted surveys and recording voice responses. That is useful when you have short gaps in your day.

Can you join both?

You can consider more than one panel, subject to their terms. First complete one registration and verification process; then add another if you want more potential invitations. Answer every screener accurately, and avoid duplicate accounts.

How to compare your results fairly

Record money actually received, completed-study minutes and unsuccessful screening minutes for each panel over the same period. Keep welcome bonuses separate from recurring survey earnings. Use the free survey earnings calculator to compare effective hourly returns.

Three steps to start receiving opportunities

  1. Open the provider’s signup page. Check that your profession and country qualify before registering.
  2. Complete your profile and verification. Use accurate credentials and follow the provider’s verification instructions.
  3. Check for a confirmation email. Finish any activation steps, then review matching invitations. Registering alone does not guarantee a study or reward.

For a promotional offer, save the terms shown at signup and complete its required action before the deadline. Do not create a duplicate account.
