About Healthcare Survey Guide

An independent directory designed to make healthcare market-research opportunities easier to compare without hiding referral relationships.

How information is handled

Healthcare Survey Guide combines first-party company information with clearly labeled contributor observations. Company policies such as eligibility, payment methods, and published bonuses are linked back to company sources when possible. Subjective judgments about survey frequency, honorarium quality, and screening experience are treated as contributor experience—not universal facts.

Information was last reviewed on August 30, 2026. Programs, geography, eligibility, compensation, referral incentives, and payout methods can change, so users should verify current terms during registration.

Referral disclosure

Many sign-up links on this site are referral links. If you register through one, the site owner may receive compensation or another referral benefit under that company's current program. Referral links are marked in the HTML as sponsored/no-follow links. A company does not need a referral program to be eligible for editorial coverage.

Editorial principles

  • No guaranteed-income claims.
  • No invented first-hand experiences or fabricated reviews.
  • Clear labeling when information is contributor-reported rather than company-verified.
  • Direct official sources used where practical.
  • Corrections when material program terms change.
