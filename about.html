About Healthcare Survey Guide

An independent directory designed to make healthcare market-research opportunities easier to compare without hiding how referral links work.

How companies are included

We include platforms that publicly describe healthcare-focused market research, paid medical surveys, clinician panels or related research opportunities. We prioritize first-party company sources when describing eligibility and services. We do not rank a company higher simply because it offers a referral program.

Company descriptions were last checked against public first-party information on August 14, 2026. Programs, geography, eligibility and compensation can change, so users should verify current terms directly with each company.

Referral disclosure

Some sign-up links may be referral links. If you register through a referral link, the site owner may receive compensation or another referral benefit under that company's program. Referral links will be labeled and technically marked as sponsored links. Inclusion in the directory is not contingent on a referral relationship.

Editorial principles

  • No fabricated earnings averages or guaranteed-income claims.
  • No fake reviews or invented first-hand experience.
  • Clear distinction between company-provided facts and editorial guidance.
  • Direct links to official sources so readers can verify current information.
  • Corrections are made when source information changes.
