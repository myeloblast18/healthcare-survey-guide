Beyond physicians

Paid healthcare surveys for nurses, NPs, PAs, pharmacists and other HCPs

Healthcare market research is not limited to doctors. Eligibility depends on the panel and the specific study.

Which healthcare professionals can be recruited?

Depending on the research question, studies may seek registered nurses, nurse practitioners, physician assistants, pharmacists, dentists, residents, fellows, veterinarians, allied health professionals, administrators and other healthcare roles. Sermo's current survey information explicitly lists several of these groups, while other platforms describe broader healthcare-professional panels.

Why invitation volume may differ by role

Market-research samples are designed around a study's decision-makers and users. One study may need pharmacists who dispense a particular category of medication; another may need nurses in a specific care setting; another may recruit NPs or PAs who independently manage patients. A low invitation rate on one panel does not necessarily mean the panel is inactive.

Questions to ask before registering

  • Does the panel accept my profession and country?
  • Will I need to verify a license, employer or professional identity?
  • How and when is compensation described?
  • What research formats are offered—surveys, interviews, communities, focus groups or other studies?
  • How does the company handle personal and professional data?
