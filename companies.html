M3 Global Research

M3 Global Research describes itself as a large global panel of verified physicians and healthcare professionals. Its U.S. site advertises paid market-research studies across a range of medical specialties.

Good fit to investigate if: you are a physician or other healthcare professional who wants a healthcare-specific research panel rather than a general consumer survey site.

Visit M3 official site

Sermo

Sermo publicly lists paid survey eligibility across a broad set of healthcare roles, including doctors, advanced practice providers, pharmacists, nurses, residents, fellows and other healthcare professionals. It combines research opportunities with a professional community.

Good fit to investigate if: you want a panel that explicitly discusses multiple HCP professions rather than physicians alone.

Visit Sermo survey page

All Global Circle

All Global Circle connects healthcare professionals with market-research studies in selected regions and notes that professional verification is important to research quality.

Good fit to investigate if: you are in one of its supported markets and want another healthcare-specific source of research invitations.

Visit All Global Circle

MDForLives

MDForLives describes research formats including surveys, polls, interviews and healthcare studies. Exact eligibility depends on the research project.

Good fit to investigate if: you are interested in formats beyond short online questionnaires.

Visit MDForLives

ZoomRx

ZoomRx has published information for doctors and healthcare professionals about joining a verified research panel for paid medical surveys.

Good fit to investigate if: you want another clinician-focused research panel to add alongside larger platforms.

Visit ZoomRx information

How to compare healthcare survey panels

For each panel, check six things before registering: whether your healthcare role is eligible, which countries are supported, how professional credentials are verified, what study formats are offered, how compensation is disclosed before participation, and how the company handles privacy. Invitation volume is inherently variable, so treat claims of guaranteed earnings cautiously.

Source methodology: Descriptions above were checked against first-party company pages available on August 14, 2026. We intentionally avoid unsourced claims about “typical” earnings or survey frequency because those can vary substantially.
