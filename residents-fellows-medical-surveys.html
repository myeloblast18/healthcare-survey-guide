Paid medical surveys for residents, fellows, and board-eligible physicians

Trainees frequently ask whether physician survey panels accept them. Eligibility is panel- and study-specific, but some platforms explicitly recruit residents.

Best documented starting point: Sermo

Sermo currently has a dedicated page for physician residents and explicitly states that residents are eligible to receive survey invitations matched to specialty and experience level. That does not mean every study will accept residents.

Why residents get screened out more often

Many studies target clinicians with minimum years in independent practice, defined patient volume, prescribing authority, or specific treatment experience.

Do not change your answers to pass screeners. Misrepresenting credentials, practice history, patient volume, or clinical experience can violate panel rules and undermine the research.
