Professional communities + incentives

Healthcare professional communities with rewards, surveys, and referral bonuses

Not every compensated healthcare platform is primarily a survey panel. These communities focus on clinician discussion and peer expertise, with incentives or research opportunities layered in.

Bonus terms change. The buttons below are referral links. Check the actual registration screen before relying on a quoted sign-up reward.

Primum

Verified U.S. healthcare providersOncology-focusedReferral-specific incentive reported

Primum is an oncology-focused professional platform that connects verified U.S. healthcare providers with more than 100 oncology subspecialists for secure one-to-one clinical questions. Its public site says responses are guaranteed within 24 hours and that the service is free for verified U.S. healthcare providers.

Referral incentive reported to this directory: $100 after asking a qualifying question within 30 days of joining. Because that incentive is not stated on Primum’s public marketing page, treat it as referral-specific and confirm the offer shown during registration before relying on it.

Eligibility: Primum’s current terms say Provider Users must be currently licensed physicians or allied health professionals actively engaged in direct clinical care. The contributor reports broader friendliness to some trainees/board-eligible clinicians; the platform’s actual eligibility rules control.

Community vs. paid survey panel: what is the difference?

A paid healthcare survey panel primarily recruits HCPs into market-research studies. A professional community primarily facilitates clinician discussion or expert exchange and may add surveys, activity rewards, referral incentives, or other compensated opportunities. If your main goal is survey honoraria, see the paid medical survey comparison.
